Building intelligent, production-ready AI infrastructure and high-throughput backends.

Aman Sharma Disha College · Noventra Labs

Hi, I'm Aman — an AI Systems and Backend Engineer based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. Currently pursuing my Bachelor's in Computer Applications at Disha College (2024–2027), I focus on the intersection of autonomous multi-agent architectures, prefix-caching latency optimization, and high-concurrency backends using FastAPI, Go, and uv.

I co-founded Noventra Labs to build autonomous agentic workflows and production-grade RAG systems. I regularly compete in hackathons — including building the Parivesh 3.0 environmental clearance engine at IIIT Naya Raipur's e-summit, the CampusFix 500+ QPS platform for GDG Solution Challenge, and SkillSnap for The Forge Hackathon.

Certified in AWS Generative AI, MongoDB Python Developer Path, Postman Student Expert, and Machine Learning Fundamentals. I believe software should be fast, deterministic, and built to solve real-world problems.
